Welcome to the second issue of the HiNAS Newsletter.

This month, we're sharing the operating data behind HiNAS Control's fuel-saving performance — how the results were measured, and what they could mean for your fleet.

These results were derived from pilot operations across vessels in active service and assessed using a methodology independently verified by ABS.

Performance by Vessel Type

Performance varied by vessel type, with savings reaching 5.4% for PCTCs and 5.2% for VLOCs in pilot operations.

5.4%
PCTCs
Pure Car and Truck Carriers operating on optimized RPM schedules derived by HiNAS Control.
  
5.2%
VLOCs
Very Large Ore Carriers on long-haul bulk routes with high schedule margin potential.

Every vessel operates under different conditions, but the data shows a consistent trend: voyage optimization can generate meaningful fuel savings in real-world service.

Turning Recorded Video into Better Decisions
 
Glovis Sunlight · Pyeongtaek Port
Navigation Camera Supports a Departure Decision

In early September 2026, during departure from Pyeongtaek Port, the crew of Glovis Sunlight needed to confirm whether the vessel had made contact with nearby fishing nets. By downloading and reviewing footage recorded by the HiNAS Navigation Camera, operators were able to assess the situation directly and reach a timely, well-informed decision — a clear example of how continuous onboard video recording delivers practical value beyond navigation alone.
